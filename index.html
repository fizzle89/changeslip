You did the extra work and got the ticket signed. Changeslip turns it into a clean, priced change-order request your GC can sign off: ticket details, priced lines from your approved rate card, the GC's own markup terms, and a packet PDF listing your backup.

What it does: prices the lines you type against your rate card and markup terms, checks the evidence a GC expects (signed ticket, receipts, dates), and exports a packet PDF when a person approves the amounts.
